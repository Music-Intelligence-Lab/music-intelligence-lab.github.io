## Opportunities

We welcome MS and PhD applicants at the American University of Beirut interested in machine learning for music, embodied musical interfaces, or computational musicology of the Arab region. Undergraduate students can get involved through the [Vertically Integrated Project Program (VIPP)](https://www.josephbakarji.com/articles/vertically-integrated-program).

For enquiries, email [jb50@aub.edu.lb](mailto:jb50@aub.edu.lb).
